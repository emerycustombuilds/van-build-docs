# Van Build Technical Docs

Welcome to the Emery Custom Builds technical reference library. Whether you're planning a full custom van conversion or just trying to understand how electrical and plumbing systems work in a camper van, you'll find practical, real-world information here.

## What You'll Find

These docs cover the technical side of van conversions — the stuff that matters when you're actually building or speccing out a rig:

- **Electrical Systems** — Load calculations, solar sizing, battery bank design, wiring best practices, and common mistakes that cost people money.
- **Plumbing & Water** — Fresh water, grey water, tank sizing, pump selection, and material choices that hold up on the road.
- **Build Process & Planning** — How professional van builds actually work, realistic budgets by tier, and timeline expectations.
- **Tools & Resources** — Our electrical system sizing calculator documentation and other reference material.

## Who This Is For

- People researching a custom van build and want to understand the technical details before committing
- DIY builders who want professional-grade reference material
- Emery Custom Builds clients who want to understand what's going into their rig
- Anyone comparing van platforms (Sprinter, ProMaster, Transit) and trying to figure out what makes sense
